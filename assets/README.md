# User-supplied assets

The audio banks, WAVs, note manifests and artwork in this directory were supplied or prepared from projects supplied by Mulú for BAILE. They are not relicensed under the code's GPL or the SDK's license. Their presence does not grant an independent right to redistribute or reuse the samples/artwork outside this authorized project.

- Tamborzão: supplied R8 drum rack and original two-bar clip.
- Voltmix and Bolha: supplied Ableton drum racks, approved note-to-pad mapping and grid quantization.
- Boot and title art: supplied BAILE mockups; original references and cropped titles preserved.

`audio/racks.json` and `audio/tamborzao.json` retain provenance, source hashes and clip events. `docs/evidence/baseline.json` records hashes of this imported asset snapshot. No private device backup or raw Ableton project is included.
